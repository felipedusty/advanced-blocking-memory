Wide-key experiments, 2026-11-02. Same lists and harness as hash-risk-20261102.txt (lab/hash-risk/Program.cs).
Lane A is the patch's FNV-1a (prime 1099511628211). Lane B is FNV-1a with the multiplier 0x9E3779B97F4A7C15, unless the section says otherwise.
The first pair128 section ('lane B: FNV-1a with another basis') is the rejected design: the same multiplier with another basis is NOT independent.
Every width row below uses lane B = the golden-ratio multiplier. The 'u128' row of hash-risk-20261102.txt used an xorshift lane (not a validated hash) and is superseded by these rows. 'dict96' is Dictionary<ulong,uint> (64-bit key plus 32 bits of lane B); 'tuple3' is (uint,uint,uint).
